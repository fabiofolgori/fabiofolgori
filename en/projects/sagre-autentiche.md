# Sagre Autentiche

*Web portal · Custom WordPress*

In just a few years Sagre Autentiche has become a reference for finding food festivals in Italy. With [Cube ProEmotion](https://www.cubeproemotion.it/) I built the portal in custom WordPress: Airbnb-style search engine, map, area for organisers, regional pages and SEO structured data.

**Technologies:** WordPress · Gutenberg blocks · ACF · Search engine · Map · Structured data
