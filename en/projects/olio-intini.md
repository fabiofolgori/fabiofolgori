# Olio Intini

*E-commerce · Multilingual WordPress*

Intini is one of the most awarded olive oil producers in Apulia, Flos Olei Farm of the Year 2020 and 2022. On the design by [Humus Design](https://humusdesign.com/it/) I built the multilingual WordPress site with online shop: years later it still holds up against today's websites.

**Technologies:** WordPress · Gutenberg blocks · ACF · E-commerce · WooCommerce · Multilingual
