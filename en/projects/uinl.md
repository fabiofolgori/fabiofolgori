# UINL, International Union of Notaries

*International organisation · WordPress and ACF*

The UINL brings together the notariats of member countries. On the design by [Marzia Bianchi](https://mbianchi.it) I developed the institutional WordPress site with custom ACF blocks: rigorous in its content, pleasant to look at and updated independently by the staff.

**Technologies:** WordPress · ACF · Gutenberg blocks
