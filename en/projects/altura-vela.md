# Altura Vela

*Sailing school · Custom management system*

Altura Vela is a sailing school in Rome. A redesign grew into a custom management system in Nuxt 4 on REST APIs: fleet calendar, areas for members and skippers, contracts, payments and AI. The school runs three times the boats it managed by hand.

**Technologies:** Nuxt 4 · REST API · Stripe · AI
