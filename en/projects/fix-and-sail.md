# Fix and Sail

*Boating platform · Nuxt and Strapi*

Fix and Sail is a platform where you recreate your boat digitally, with model and accessories, to keep track of maintenance and deadlines. It was born from a private client's idea and is now in the launch phase. I develop the application in Nuxt on Strapi REST APIs, from Figma to a catalogue with AI recognition of parts from a photo.

**Technologies:** Nuxt · Strapi · REST API · Figma · AI
