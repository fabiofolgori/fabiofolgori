# Dotcampus

*Hotel and coliving · Block-based WordPress*

For Dotcampus, a business & student hotel in Rome, I turned the concept and visual design by [Marzia Bianchi](https://mbianchi.it) into a custom WordPress site (block-based with ACF), so even the most complex layouts can be managed straight from the dashboard in minutes.

**Technologies:** WordPress · Gutenberg blocks · ACF
