# Arsity

*Culture · WordPress with map*

Arsity covers exhibitions and art spaces in Rome. With [We Meet Brands](https://www.wemeetbrands.com/), who created a typographic, minimal design, I built the website in WordPress: browsing across exhibitions and venues, and a map to find them. Years later it still looks current.

**Technologies:** WordPress · Gutenberg blocks · ACF · Map
